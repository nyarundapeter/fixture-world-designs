TS-01

Kinungi Bungalow

Residential redesign · Kinungi, Nairobi–Naivasha Highway · 3 bed, all ensuite

SheetsBubble · GA ×2 · S-01 · IE ×2ToolsArchiCAD, Twinmotion
Problem statement

The existing plan (14.76 m overall) was congested: undersized rooms and no clear circulation. The survey drawing set the problem statement; the redesign applies Universal Design guidelines so the house needs no adaptive features later.

Programme after redesign
  • Master bedroom + 2 bedrooms, each ensuite
  • Study, laundry, pantry
  • Open living / dining with L-shaped kitchen and island
  • Entrance hall as the social–private hinge (see bubble diagram)

TS-06

The Loop

Workplace concept · creative sustainable office for a public-sector design department · interior, graphic and product teams

SheetsAnalysis ×8 · Programme · GA ×4Capacity≈ 60 staff
Site inventory
  • First floor, multi-storey commercial building; stair and lift from lobby
  • Flat terrain on an elevated hill site; commercial and retail neighbours
  • Concrete walls; aluminium-glass partitions; exposed beam-and-slab ceiling
  • Double sliding windows; mahogany doors; tiled floors
  • Marked exits, smoke detectors, extinguishers, lift access
Climate & sensory
  • Vertical shading to E/W glazing against low-angle glare
  • Workstations to the well-lit north façade; meeting, storage and services to darker façades
  • Overhangs for high sun; operable SE windows, high-level openings opposite for cross-ventilation
  • Road and court noise: acoustic panels, soft furnishing, window seals
  • Frosted lower films or planters to screen traffic views
Audit findings
  • Desks too densely packed; no transition zones; no growth capacity
  • Circulation crosses quiet work zones
  • Too few desks for 60 people; worn furniture; no integrated storage
  • Broken blinds; dated fluorescent fittings; no acoustic treatment
  • Too few outlets, cable clutter; uneven AC coverage
