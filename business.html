Residential

Kinungi Bungalow

Kinungi, Nairobi–Naivasha Highway3 bedrooms, all ensuiteRenovation and redesign
The problem
The existing house felt congested. Rooms were cramped and moving between them was awkward.
What we did
Re-planned the bungalow into three ensuite bedrooms, a study, a laundry and an open living, dining and kitchen wing. Every room was sized for easy movement, so no later adaptations are needed as the family ages.
The result
A bright, quiet home with polished mahogany and parquet floors, a tray ceiling in the living room and large windows facing the garden.

Bedrooms

Polished wood parquet for warmth underfoot, with daylight in every room.

Bathrooms

Slip-resistant porcelain floors and marble walls that resist water and cleaning chemicals.

Kitchen

An L-shaped layout with an island makes the most of the floor area.

Hospitality

The Burger Joint

Kinungi township, Naivasha–Nakuru highwayRestaurant, snack bar and butchery
The problem
Snack shop, butchery, dining room and kitchen sat in separate small rooms joined by a narrow passage. Customers and staff kept crossing paths.
What we did
Opened the plan into one dining hall with the kitchen, snack counter and butchery placed along its edges, so food travels a short, clear route.
The result
A bright roadside stop with warm wood floors, brass chandeliers, a raised white-marble counter and easy-to-clean, slip-resistant kitchen floors.

Leisure

Hyper Game Zone

Westlands, NairobiBowling, pool, table tennis, video gamesKids’ play area
The problem
A recreation centre that should welcome everyone, from small children to wheelchair users, without losing its energy.
What we did
Gave each game its own zone and themed wall graphic, linked by wide, step-free circulation. Glass and ambient light make the indoor halls feel larger.
The result
Bold colour where it counts, non-slip ceramic floors throughout and a calm lounge for parents and spectators.

Culture

Art Gallery Pavilion

Circular gallery pavilionReception, two galleries, office, store
The brief
An engaging, practical interior that presents artwork well and gives visitors a comfortable visit.
What we did
A neutral, uncluttered backdrop with flexible display walls, adjustable lighting, a branded welcome area, a private viewing room and rest seating.
The result
A gallery that adapts to each exhibition, with secure back-of-house storage for handling artwork.

Workplace

Gallery Offices and Boardrooms

Multi-storey office buildingExecutive offices, workstation pools, boardrooms, training hall
The brief
A productive, comfortable workplace for gallery staff, curators and management that still feels connected to the gallery.
What we did
Open workstations alongside private meeting rooms, ergonomic furniture, daylight plus task lighting, sound-absorbing partitions and art-led accent walls.
The result
Boardrooms, a mothers’ room, accessible washrooms and a coffee station, all within a clear, easy-to-find layout.

Workplace concept

The Loop

Creative, sustainable design studioInterior, graphic and product design teamsAbout 60 staff
The problem
Desks packed too tightly, noise from a busy road and courts nearby, broken blinds and no room to grow.
The idea
“Where ideas evolve without end.” The studio is laid out as a loop: inspiration, ideation, development, feedback, presentation, and back again.
Sustainability
Bamboo flooring, carpet tiles, low-VOC paint, recycled gypsum partitions and an acoustic ceiling.

How we work

From first visit to finished room

Five stages, in this order. You approve each one before the next begins, and you see the finished space in 3D before any money is spent on site.

Stage 1

Listen & survey

Site visit, measurements, photographs and your wish list. We record light, noise, services and access.

Stage 2

Define the problem

We compare what exists with what you need and agree on the problems the design must solve.

Stage 3

Plan the space

How the rooms relate, then the new layout, drawn over your existing plan so the changes are clear.

Stage 4

See it in 3D

Finishes, furniture and lighting shown in photo-real renders. Revisions happen here.

Stage 5

Build-ready drawings

Plans, sections and elevations with every finish labelled, ready for your contractor.
